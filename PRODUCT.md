# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Decision makers and teams looking for a digital marketing partner. They arrive to evaluate Goya's range, point of view and proof of work before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Goya as a strategic digital marketing agency and guide qualified visitors toward an initial project consultation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic and confident. The experience should feel like a dark technical studio with controlled light, rather than a generic software company.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, cheerful stock-agency sites, beige editorial layouts, purple gradients, oversized soft cards and decorative glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

- Make the agency name and point of view immediately memorable.
- Balance cinematic atmosphere with clear, scannable information.
- Use light and cyan as signals of intent, never as wallpaper.
- Keep every marketing claim close to a meaningful next action.
- Let replacement content be simple and local.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast as a baseline; visible keyboard focus; responsive touch targets; semantic landmarks and labels; reduced motion support.
